# Insurance Risk Analysis

This project analyzes an auto-insurance fraud dataset containing 1,000 records and 39 attributes related to policy details, customer demographics, incident information, claim amounts, and vehicle data. The goal is to identify patterns that indicate fraudulent claims and support faster, smarter claims investigation.

## Business Objective
Insurance companies face financial losses due to fraudulent claims. This dashboard helps stakeholders monitor claim trends, identify fraud-prone customer profiles, and uncover geographic and time-based risk patterns.

## Key Business Questions
- Which customer profiles, policy types, and incident characteristics are most associated with fraudulent claims?
- Are there geographic or time-based bottlenecks in the claims process?
- Do higher education levels reduce the likelihood of fraud?
- Do higher claim amounts correlate with increased fraud risk?

## KPIs Covered
- Total Claims
- Fraudulent Claims
- Fraud Rate (%)
- Total and Average Claim Amounts
- Injury Claim, Property Claim, Vehicle Claim analysis

## Insights
- Fraudulent claims show significantly higher average payouts than non-fraudulent claims.
- Certain occupations, especially executive/managerial roles, show higher fraud risk.
- Fraud risk varies by hour of incident, with some time windows showing notably higher rates.
- Geographic clustering helps identify fraud hotspots for closer investigation.

## Recommendations
- Flag high-risk claims for immediate review by the Special Investigation Unit (SIU).
- Improve response time during peak fraud hours.
- Prioritize investigation in high-risk categories to reduce loss and claim leakage.
